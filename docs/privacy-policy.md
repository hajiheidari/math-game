NumCross ("the app") is a math puzzle game for people aged 13 and over, published by
[PUBLISHER NAME] ("we").

## What the app stores on your device
All of this stays on your device. We do not run servers and receive none of it.
- **Age group** (13–15, 16–17 or 18+). The app asks your age once. Only the resulting age
  group is saved; your age and birth date are never stored or sent anywhere.
- If the age entered is under 13, the app stores only a flag that blocks play, and nothing else.
- Game progress: levels completed, stars, daily puzzles solved, streaks, unfinished puzzles,
  hint coins.
- Settings: language and digit style.
- Whether you bought "Remove ads".

Deleting the app (or its data) deletes all of this.

## Advertising
The app shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the
advertising ID), IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. See
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.
- In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first (Google's consent form).
  You can change your choice any time in **Settings → Privacy choices**.
- On iOS, Apple asks whether the app may track you; the game works the same either way.
- Ads are limited to content rated for teens. For the 13–17 age groups, ad requests are
  marked for teen treatment, and for the 13–15 group as under the age of consent
  (non-personalized ads).

## Purchases
"Remove ads" is bought through the App Store or Google Play. We do not see or store your
payment details.

## Analytics, accounts, sharing
The app has no analytics, no accounts, no chat or social features, and does not sell or share
personal data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Children
The app is not intended for children under 13 and blocks play when an age under 13 is entered.
