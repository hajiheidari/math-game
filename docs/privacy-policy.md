_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

Adadi ("the app") is a math puzzle game for people aged 13 and over, published by
Amir HajiHeidari ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## Summary
- We do not run servers, accounts or analytics. We receive no data about you.
- Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
- Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which may collect device data as described below. You can
  remove ads with a one-time purchase.

## Information stored on your device
The following is saved locally on your device only. We cannot see it, and it is never sent to us.
- **Age group** (13–15, 16–17 or 18+). The app asks your age once to choose suitable puzzles and
  ad settings. Only the resulting age group is saved; your age or birth date is never stored or
  sent anywhere.
- If an age under 13 is entered, the app stores only a flag that blocks play, and nothing else.
- **Game progress:** levels completed, stars, daily puzzles solved, streaks, unfinished puzzles,
  hint coins.
- **Settings:** language, digit style, sound and vibration on/off, whether the tutorial was
  completed.
- Whether you bought "Remove ads".

Deleting the app, or clearing its data, permanently deletes all of this. There is nothing for us
to delete on our side.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, limit and
measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID,
IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this
information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. How
Google uses information from apps that use its services:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland):** before any ad is requested, you are asked for consent
  through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in
  **Settings → Privacy choices**.
- **iOS tracking:** on iPhone and iPad, Apple asks whether the app may track you. The game works
  the same whichever you choose.
- **Teen-appropriate ads:** all ads are limited to content rated for teens. For players in the
  13–17 age groups, ad requests are marked for teen treatment, and for the 13–15 group as under
  the age of consent, which means non-personalized ads.
- You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your
  device settings.

## Purchases
"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store
processes the payment; we do not receive or store your payment details. The app only remembers on
your device that the purchase was made, and can restore it through the store.

## What we do not do
The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics or crash reporting, no chat or social
features, and no location access. We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and we do
not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Children
The app is not intended for children under 13. It blocks play when an age under 13 is entered
and stores nothing about that person except the block. If you believe a child under 13 is using
the app, deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Your rights
Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or
delete. All app data is on your device and under your control (see above). For data processed by
Google for advertising, use the Privacy choices option in the app, your device's ad settings, or
contact Google.

## Changes to this policy
If the app changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated"
date above.
